# Headset Desk 0.5.0 Beta — Windows 11 design, CH720N fix, more controls

A new look that follows Windows 11 conventions, a fix for swapped noise modes on the WH-CH720N, and the first extra XM5 controls.

## Fixed

- **WH-CH720N: Ambient and Noise Cancelling were swapped.** On this model the ambient bit is the opposite of the XM5, so tapping Ambient gave noise cancelling. The app now reads and writes it the right way round for the CH720N. (From an owner report; please confirm on your unit.)
- **Allow changes no longer switches itself off after a normal change.** The same mistake made the readback check fail, and any failed check turned the switch off. Now the app waits up to about 2.5 seconds for the headphones to settle, and a readback that does not match is reported ("The headphones reported X instead of Y") while the switch stays on. A lost connection, a missing ACK or a refusal still turns it off. Nothing is ever resent.

## New design

- Windows 11 layout: a left navigation pane (Headphones, Sound, Features, Device) that collapses to an icon rail and then to a menu button as the window narrows, settings cards, Segoe UI Variable type sizes, 4 px control corners and the Windows light and dark colours. The title bar takes the page colour and follows light/dark mode.
- A larger device card: the headphone drawing inside a battery ring. Rings drift inward for Noise Cancelling and outward for Ambient (still when Windows animation effects are off).
- Noise control as three tiles with icons, plus an Ambient level slider.
- Equalizer with a curve that runs through the slider handles, dB scale and frequency labels.
- Messages appear in a bar that you can close; routine confirmations clear after a few seconds.

## New controls (experimental)

All of these follow the same rules as noise and EQ: off until **Allow changes** is on for the connection, one validated command, ACK, fresh readback.

- **Voice passthrough** while Ambient is on.
- **Speak-to-Chat** (WH-1000XM5).
- **DSEE Extreme** (XM5) / **DSEE** (CH720N).

Speak-to-Chat and DSEE are read with two extra queries that are only sent after you turn Allow changes on. They use the imported protocol code; no hardware capture has verified them yet, so write confidence stays UNKNOWN. A feature the headphones do not report stays unavailable.

Not included yet: multipoint, touch controls, wearing detection, Adaptive Sound Control, 360 Reality Audio and auto power-off. There is no verified protocol evidence for them, and auto power-off needs its option mapping checked first.

## Unchanged

Controls start off and switch off on disconnect, device change, reconnect or a link failure. EQ edits stay local until **Apply**. Close hides to the tray; tray **Quit** exits. One headset session at a time. No firmware updates, power commands, cloud, analytics or startup entry.

## How this build was made

Built and checked by the [desktop workflow](https://github.com/ashwinpr15/headset-desk/actions/workflows/desktop-release.yml) on GitHub's Windows runners with Qt 6.8.3 (mingw_64) and MinGW GCC 13.1.0: offline protocol, control and QML tests, simulated screenshots with only the bundled runtime on PATH, then a silent install, hash check, launch and uninstall of the installer. No commands were sent to physical headphones during development.

Independent project; not affiliated with Sony. App and imported Sony Device Center code: MIT. Qt 6.8.3: dynamically linked LGPLv3 with runtime notices included.
